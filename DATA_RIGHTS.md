# Data and Source Rights

The MIT licence applies to CPT-authored software, not third-party publications,
publisher figures, source workbooks, fonts or database content.

CPT-authored annotations and calculated results explicitly released by CPT
are offered under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit Carbon Property Tables and retain the original source citations and
release identifiers. This grant covers only rights held by the CPT authors.
It does not relicense underlying papers, source compilations or third-party
database rights. Original sources retain their own terms. Arimo fonts retain
their bundled SIL Open Font License.

Institutional access is not permission to redistribute a paper or train a model
on it. CPT's public tools provide bibliographic locations and bounded
calculations; they do not distribute source documents or searchable full text.
Users must obtain source access and any required reuse permissions themselves.

Acquisition archives, extracted full text, validation workbooks and receipts,
and complete serving tables are private backend assets, not part of this
software repository or its distributable packages. MatWeb-attributed records
are withheld pending independently verified replacement sources with suitable
reuse terms. A citation to a secondary source that itself uses MatWeb does not
remove that restriction.

Current-tree removal does not erase earlier Git commits, forks or downloads.
Historical removal and archive deposits require a separate reviewed procedure.
